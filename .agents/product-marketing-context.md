# Ratunda — Product Marketing Context

## Company Overview
- **Brand**: Ratunda Renovasi
- **Legal Entity**: PT. Pencipta Organik Imaji
- **Founded**: 2024
- **Type**: Home repair & renovation service company
- **Parent**: Sister company of **Poiesis**, an architecture & design firm with 10+ years experience in residential and commercial projects
- **Website**: ratunda.id
- **Instagram**: @ratunda.id

## Unique Value Proposition
- **"One Project, One Architect In-Charge"** — every project is led by a dedicated architect who consults with the client and supervises the workers (tukang & mandor) on site
- Affordable yet quality home repair, backed by architectural expertise from Poiesis
- Transparent pricing via RAB (Rencana Anggaran Biaya) presentation
- Addresses common renovation pain points: work not meeting expectations, scope creep without clarity, irresponsible workers, below-standard quality

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Homeowners in Jabodetabek (Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, Bekasi) and major cities across Indonesia
- **Demographics**: Middle to upper-middle class, own their home, need repairs or renovations
- **Pain Points**:
  - Pekerjaan tidak sesuai ekspektasi (work doesn't match expectations)
  - Scope bertambah terus tanpa kejelasan/dasar teknis (scope creep without technical basis)
  - Tukang tidak bertanggung jawab (irresponsible workers)
  - Kualitas dibawah standar (below-standard quality)

## Brand Voice & Image
- Friendly, approachable (bersahabat, tidak ribet)
- Trusted and responsible (trusted dan bertanggung jawab)
- Professional but not intimidating
- Language: Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)

## Core Services (9 Main Categories — Problem-Oriented)
1. **Atap Bocor** — leaky roof, cracked tiles, clogged drainage, fallen roof
2. **Ganti Lampu / Electrical** — switches, smart systems, cable installation, CCTV, MCB
3. **Renovasi Interior** — wall repainting, new plafond, ceramic, bathroom, terrace, ventilation
4. **Pasang Kanopi** — railings, fences, doors, windows, glass doors, skylight
5. **Pasang Pompa dan AC** — water pump, booster, water tank, pipes, water heater, faucet
6. **Bangun Dapur** — home extension, 2nd floor, concrete/steel stairs, carport, garden, exhaust
7. **Pemasangan Roster** — tiles, marble, mosaic, wood panels, secondary skin, decorative walls
8. **Membuat Furniture** — custom cabinets, sofas, work desks, TV racks, kitchen cabinets
9. **Cor Dak Beton** — column reinforcement, additional foundation, concrete deck, steel structure

## Business Process Flow
1. **Understand & Contact** — client discovers Ratunda via Instagram, landing page, Google, or referrals → contacts via WhatsApp
2. **Connect & Survey** — first call with Architect In-Charge → on-site survey and measurement
3. **Present RAB** — detailed cost breakdown (scope, materials, estimated cost, timeline) presented online
4. **Deal & Contract** — negotiation, agreement on cost and timeline, contract signing, initial invoice
5. **Repair & Renovation** — architect-led execution, daily WA group updates on cost and progress

## Competitive Differentiators
- Backed by 10+ years of architectural expertise (via Poiesis)
- Architect-supervised, not just "tukang" work
- Transparent RAB before work begins
- Daily progress updates via WhatsApp group
- Warranty on completed work (garansi pekerjaan)
