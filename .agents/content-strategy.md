# Ratunda Renovasi — Content Strategy

## 3 Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Biaya & Perencanaan Renovasi
- #1 question homeowners have before hiring
- Drives leads to free consultation + RAB
- Keywords: biaya renovasi rumah, biaya renovasi dapur, biaya cor dak beton

### Pillar 2: Masalah Rumah & Solusi
- Problem-oriented (matches Ratunda's 9 service categories)
- Each article maps to a specific service
- Keywords: cara mengatasi atap bocor, dinding lembab, renovasi kamar mandi

### Pillar 3: Tips Memilih Jasa Renovasi
- Trust-building, differentiates from "tukang biasa"
- Positions architect-led model as the answer
- Keywords: tips memilih jasa renovasi, RAB renovasi, tukang vs kontraktor
